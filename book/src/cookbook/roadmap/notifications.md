# Get owner notifications on the desktop

**Not yet available.** Planned for v1 ([roadmap](../../../../README.md#roadmap)).

The service tells you about approvals, vault changes, failures and recovery reminders on your desktop, and writes
them to the log where there is no desktop.

Until then, these appear only in the audit log; see [investigate with the audit log](../investigate-audit-log.md).
